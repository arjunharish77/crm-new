### 0.3 Google SEO guidance — verified against the actual current docs, not just the summary
I had an independent pass fetch and read the six Google Search Central pages `google_seo.md` cites, to check the summary for drift. It's largely accurate, with four corrections worth knowing before you read §5:

1. **It's "Course list" structured data, not a generic "Course" type** (docs page title is literally "Course list"). It requires a **minimum of three courses** in an `ItemList`, each with `name`, `description` (≤60 characters, display-truncated), and recommended `provider`. There is no `CourseInstance`/`hasCourseInstance` requirement mentioned anywhere on the current page — that's sometimes cited for a *different*, older individual-course schema and would be a wrong thing to build here.
2. **"FAQPage" is not in Google's current structured-data gallery at all** — the closest listed types are "Q&A" (generic) and "Education Q&A" (specifically flashcard-style student Q&A, not general FAQs). This doesn't mean drop FAQ schema — it costs nothing and still feeds AI-answer extraction — but don't expect a guaranteed rich-snippet from it, and I want to be honest that this isn't a Google-endorsed rich-result type by that name today. (This matches the honest framing I already used in `22_UNNATIVIDYA_PLATFORM_ENHANCEMENTS_PLAN.md` §3 — consistent, not new information, just re-confirmed against the primary source.)
3. **Google explicitly rules out "AI SEO" markup.** Direct quotes from the AI-optimization guide: *"You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, markup, or Markdown"* (this rules out `llms.txt`, which is not a real Google-recognized file), *"There's no requirement to break your content into tiny pieces,"* *"Structured data isn't required for generative AI search, and there's no special schema.org markup you need to add."* Anything in this plan billed as "AI SEO" is really just: be crawlable, be original, meet normal technical requirements. I've scoped §5.6 accordingly — no invented AI-specific tactics.
4. **Google-Extended (AI-training crawler control) is a separate lever from AI Overviews appearance** — conflating the two is a common mistake. Not urgent for this site, noted for completeness in §5.6.
