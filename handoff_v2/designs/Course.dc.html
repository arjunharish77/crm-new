About this program

Online MBA from Manipal University Jaipur is a 24-month, UGC-entitled online degree designed for graduates and working professionals who want flexible learning, online proctored assessments, and career-focused support without relocating. Live weekend classes are backed by recorded lectures, a digital library and faculty office hours.
