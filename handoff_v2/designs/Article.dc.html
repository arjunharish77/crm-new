Is an online MBA valid for government jobs?

RD
Ritika Desai
Senior education counsellor, UnnatiVidya

Short answer: yes — if the degree is UGC-entitled. Under the UGC (Online Degrees) Regulations, a degree earned fully online from an entitled university is legally equivalent to the same degree earned on campus, for every purpose: government recruitment, PSU jobs, higher studies and state services.

The one check that matters

Before enrolling, open the UGC-DEB portal and confirm the university is entitled to offer that specific program online for the current academic year. Entitlement is per-program and per-year — a university can be entitled for its MBA but not its MCA.

What the certificate looks like

Entitled universities issue the same certificate to online and on-campus learners — same seal, same signatures, no mention of the mode. Recruiters cannot distinguish delivery mode from the document, and legally may not treat it differently.

Where candidates still get rejected

Rejections almost always trace back to non-entitled institutions, lapsed entitlements, or "degrees" that were actually diplomas. If the price looks impossible and the institution is not on the UGC-DEB list, walk away.
