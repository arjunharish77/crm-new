How we verify every number on this site

Fees, approvals and placement claims are copied from official sources, dated, and re-checked each admission cycle. Where a figure is indicative rather than official, we say so on the page.

The four checks

1
UGC-DEB entitlement
Every program is matched by name against the current UGC-DEB entitlement notification. Entitlement is per program and per academic year, so last year's approval is not evidence.
2
Fee from the university's own page
Total fee, semester fee and EMI come from the official program page, not from aggregator listings. The source URL sits at the bottom of every course page.
3
Eligibility and specialisation audit
Each course is tagged as source-verified or inherited from a stream-level default. Verified fields carry a badge on the page so you know which is which.
4
Placement claims kept honest
Universities offer placement assistance, not guaranteed jobs. We repeat their wording and never convert an assistance rate into a placement guarantee.

What we will not publish

✕Salary figures with no stated source. Career bands on this site are labelled indicative.
✕"Starting from" fees that exclude exam or convocation charges.
✕Programs from institutions absent from the UGC-DEB list, at any commission.
✕Rankings we cannot trace to a published methodology.
Found something wrong?
If a fee has changed or an entitlement has lapsed, tell us and we will correct it within one working day — and credit the correction on the page.
