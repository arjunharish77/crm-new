What I would prioritize for Unnati Vidya

If you have limited bandwidth, I wouldn't try 30 SEO tactics.

For the next 30 days, I'd concentrate on:

Week 1: Clarity + canonical audit + structured data + PageSpeed/Core Web Vitals.

Week 2: Create the 10–15 most commercially valuable comparison pages.

Week 3: Improve every major course page with official sources, updated date, author/reviewer, FAQs, fee breakdown, eligibility and internal links.

Week 4: Build one genuinely useful free tool — my first choice would be an Online Degree Fee + EMI comparison calculator — and start distributing the resulting content through LinkedIn/Instagram/YouTube/email.

The biggest mistake at this stage would be producing 100 AI-generated SEO articles. Google explicitly warns against creating lots of search-engine-first content and changing/publishing content merely to appear fresh. You have a better advantage: structured course data + comparisons + counselling knowledge + actual learner behaviour. Build around that.

And Google itself is currently able to render substantial content from your homepage and /courses, so the technical foundation isn't bad. The bigger challenge now is building enough useful, differentiated and trustworthy pages for Google to have a reason to rank Unnati Vidya over established education aggregators.
