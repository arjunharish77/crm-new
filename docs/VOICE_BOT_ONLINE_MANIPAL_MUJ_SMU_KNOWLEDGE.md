# Online Manipal voice-bot knowledge: MUJ and SMU online degrees

Verified against the linked Online Manipal program pages on **28 October 2026**. This document covers the **nine Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ)** and **nine Sikkim Manipal University (SMU)** online degrees listed on their institution pages. **MAHE is outside this knowledge base.**

Use the university and full program name together when answering. An MBA, MCA, BBA, BCom or MA at one university must not be confused with the similarly named degree at the other. The fees below are the **published Indian-national base course and semester fees**, before any offer, scholarship or financing charge; they are not a final payable quotation. Check the linked page before quoting a fee to a caller, and refresh this uploaded document whenever a fee or program changes. NRI/foreign fees and eligibility may differ and should be confirmed through the relevant international application route or by a counselor. Do not promise admission or decide an applicant's eligibility on a call.

MUJ and SMU describe their listed online degrees as **UGC-entitled** and both universities as **NAAC A+ accredited**. Online learning uses a learning management system; course pages describe live and recorded learning materials, and online examinations. Ask a counselor to confirm any program-specific attendance, examination or assessment detail. [MUJ institution page](https://www.onlinemanipal.com/institution/manipal-university-jaipur) · [SMU institution page](https://www.onlinemanipal.com/institution/sikkim-manipal-university)

## Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ)

### MUJ online Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Duration: **24 months, four semesters**. Published Indian-national fee: **₹1,84,000 total; ₹46,000 per semester**. Indian applicants need a recognized 10+2+3 bachelor's degree or accepted equivalent and at least **50% aggregate in graduation** (**45% for reserved categories**). The program offers elective specializations; the page describes a choice of one super specialization or two specializations from semester three. Confirm the current elective list before naming an option. [Official MUJ MBA page](https://www.onlinemanipal.com/online-mba-manipal-university-jaipur)

### MUJ online Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Duration: **24 months, four semesters**. Published Indian-national fee: **₹1,64,000 total; ₹41,000 per semester**. Indian applicants need a recognized 10+2+3 bachelor's degree in Computer Applications, Computer Science or IT, or another bachelor's stream with the compulsory first-semester bridge course in computer and IT. Applicants without mathematics at 10+2 or bachelor's level also need the mathematics bridge course. The listed graduation threshold is **50% aggregate**, or **45% for reserved categories**. [Official MUJ MCA page](https://www.onlinemanipal.com/online-mca-degree-muj)

### MUJ online Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

Duration: **36 months, six semesters**. Published Indian-national fee: **₹1,44,000 total; ₹24,000 per semester**. Indian applicants need recognized **10+2** education or a recognized **10+3 diploma**. The current page lists management electives, including marketing, HR, finance and accounting, entrepreneurship, analytics, retail and digital marketing; confirm availability before promising a particular elective. [Official MUJ BBA page](https://www.onlinemanipal.com/online-bba-degree-muj)

## Sikkim Manipal University (SMU)

### SMU online Master of Business Administration (MBA), dual specialization

Duration: **24 months, four semesters**. Published Indian-national fee: **₹1,20,000 total; ₹30,000 per semester**. Indian applicants need a recognized bachelor's degree in any discipline in an accepted **10+2+3, 10+2+4 or 10+3+3** pattern, or accepted equivalent, with at least **50% aggregate in graduation** (**45% for reserved categories**). Students choose two specializations; the page lists marketing, finance, HR, systems, operations and supply chain management, and healthcare.
