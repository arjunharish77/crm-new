How we verify our data

We check every fee, approval and admission date against the university's own published pages each admission cycle, and flag anything we can't independently confirm.

Our process

travel_explore
1. Collect
Fees, eligibility and approvals from official university pages.
rule
2. Cross-check
Every program against the UGC-DEB entitlement list.
flag
3. Flag
Anything we can't confirm is labelled on the page.
update
4. Re-check
Every admission cycle, and whenever a university updates its pages.

What the labels mean

SOURCE VERIFIEDMatches the university's current official page for this admission cycle.
CONFIRM WITH COUNSELLORPublished, but varies by case or session — confirm before applying.
BEING RE-CHECKEDWe're reviewing this for the upcoming cycle. Older data shown until confirmed.
