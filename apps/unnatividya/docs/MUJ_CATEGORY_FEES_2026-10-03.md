## Category and charge handling

- Keep NRI and other-nationality schedules separate; do not publish currency conversions as university fees.
- The prospectus identifies Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka for INR payments. Confirm applicable tuition for these exceptions.
- An additional non-refundable INR 500 application charge is specified for Indian applicants and the four named neighbouring-country groups. Do not infer a waiver for other groups.
- Promotions and financing terms need their own review. The domestic panels restrict combining financing with additional offers.
- Exam/resit, convocation, certificate, tax and processing charges are not resolved by this tuition comparison. Avoid “all-inclusive” claims.
