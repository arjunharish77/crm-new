Owner rules / decisions (authoritative, see docs/NEXT_PHASE_PLAN_2026-10.md §11 and docs/DESIGN_AND_CONTENT_STANDARD.md):
1. Generic CTA label is exactly "Apply now". Lead flow is contact-first: step 1 Name, Email, Phone (international codes allowed, NRI learners) + ONE required, initially UNCHECKED consent (version application-follow-up-2026-10-08-v2); step 2 Course (required) + University (optional, "No preference"); step 3 email OTP verification. Opened as a modal (data-open-lead) or /lead page. No marketing checkbox on the form (newsletter sign-up is separate: footer + article). No appointment booking.
2. NO phone number anywhere (owner removed "1800-120-4050"; contact is admin@unnatividya.com).
3. Interactive /compare tool is gated: results unlock only after form + email OTP verification. Editorial comparison pages /compare/[course]/[pair] are public.
4. No "best"/"winner"/"Lowest" highlighting in comparisons based on unverified data (rating, placement, avg package, specialisation count). Placement/package/partners are university-wide figures and must be labelled so. Differences-only toggle exists.
5. Ratings/stars stay visible (verification deferred) but no AggregateRating markup; ratings must not drive ranking/sorting/recommendations. The "highest rated"/"popular" sort was removed; default sort = relevance, then fee.
6. Template values not shown as program facts: no "Full payment (2% off)/Save 2%" plan, credits/exams/weekly hours show "Confirm with the university" unless program-specific. Application fee ₹500 only for MUJ/SMU (Indian applicants).
7. Reviews: the two generic named reviews were removed from course pages; testimonials on home are owner-retained existing content (deferred verification); NEVER invent new testimonials/reviews/stats.
8. Salary bands labelled "indicative, not verified"; recognition = per-program dated UGC-DEB 2026-27 result (Recognition section, chip in course hero); institution badges must not imply program approval.
9. Homepage owner claims: "10k+ learners guided", "No hidden charges", "lowest-fee guarantee", no-cost EMI wording, "Approvals you can check", "Support till enrolment" (documents, loans, follow-ups confirmed). Do not add new service claims (e.g. jobs, community) without owner confirmation.
10. Existing features the redesign must keep: category fee selector (Indian/NRI/Foreign) on course pages; Recognition section; source + checked-date lines; Next intake; total cost planner /tools/total-cost; eligibility checker /tools/eligibility-checker; Groq recommender (/recommender, 5 hard-filter questions + AI ranking + follow-up chat); EMI calculator; shortlist; newsletter sign-up; CMS-backed blog (/blog, /blog/[slug]) and guides (/online-degree-guides/[slug]); specialization pages (101, indexable); editorial comparisons; NIRF ranking card on university pages; course decision guidance (increment 77); SEO URLs (/courses/[slug], /universities/[slug], /specializations/[slug], /blog/[slug], /privacy, /terms, /refund-policy) must not change to query-param URLs.
11. Design standard: WCAG 2.2 AA, ≥44px targets, 16px body, reduced motion everywhere, content visible without JS (no scroll-reveal that hides content), CSP currently allows only self for fonts/styles (Google Fonts CDN would be blocked; next/font self-hosting is fine), no new big dependencies without review.
12. English only; three universities only.
