## Course page: proposed reading order

1. **Decision summary above the fold:** exact degree and awarding institution; duration and mode; total tuition with selected applicant category/currency; short eligibility summary; recognition status for the relevant session; last checked dates. One clear **View details** path and one **Apply now** entry. Do not make the learner interpret a row of badges to answer these questions.
2. **Who can apply:** separate Indian, NRI and foreign rules only where published. Present qualification, marks, subject prerequisites, bridge/entrance conditions and unresolved cases in a readable table. State that university admission is the final decision. Link the exact source beside the rule.
3. **What and how you study:** reviewed semester/term outline; electives versus required subjects; when a specialization is selected; class format, recording availability, typical scheduling, expected effort, learning-platform access and academic support **only where supported for this program**. Keep unreviewed outlines labelled “Illustrative study areas.”
4. **Assessment and exams:** assignment/internal-assessment split, exam mode, proctoring/location, attendance requirements, resit rules and result timelines where officially stated. If unknown, show a short “Confirm with university” question list rather than a generic claim.
5. **Full cost:** tuition by university-defined applicant category in original currency; payment schedule; known application, examination, convocation, tax and other mandatory charges; scholarship conditions; financing assumptions and exclusions. Distinguish “not published” from “included.” Never infer an international fee by currency conversion.
6. **Recognition and certificate:** program, mode, academic session, printed listing institution, source and checked date; institution-level accreditation in a separate subsection. Explain the five Amity UP matches with their owner-confirmed awarding attribution until independent evidence is recorded. A sample certificate needs provenance and must not promise its wording for every intake.
7. **Admission and learner support:** confirmed intake/deadline, required documents by applicant category, university submission/payment steps, refund/cancellation route, official support contacts and available career services. Separate Unnati Vidya's enquiry from the university application. Do not promise a turnaround time without an operational source.
8. **Decision help:** two or three meaningful alternatives, relevant guides or comparisons, and FAQs answering unanswered questions specific to this course. Keep deferred ratings/outcome figures clearly separate from verified program facts and out of recommendation logic.

The existing section picker should group these into about six short top-level labels on phones (for example Overview, Eligibility, Study, Cost, Recognition, Admission), with Careers and Sources reachable within those sections. Keep the summary, eligibility, tuition and recognition visible without opening an accordion or submitting a lead. Add detail below the summary; do not stack more badge rows or repeat **Apply now** after every section.

## University page: proposed reading order

1. **Institution identity:** the exact legal/awarding university, city and whether an “Online” brand is a platform or a university. This is essential for the Amity entity distinction and for separating MUJ/SMU from MAHE on Online Manipal.
2. **Programs and costs:** a searchable or filterable list of the currently published programs, grouped by level, with duration, domestic total tuition and a clear route to program-specific international/NRI fees. Do not compare “annual fee from” to total program tuition without labeling the period.
3. **Recognition explained:** institutional accreditation and ranking on one side, current-session program listing on the other. Each claim gets a source, scope and checked date. Do not infer that a university badge certifies every degree.
4. **Learning experience and support:** online class and recording model, exams, LMS, mentoring, accessibility support and career services, distinguishing university-wide statements from program-specific ones. Source any faculty profile and avoid treating a faculty roster as a promise that each person teaches each listed program.
5. **Admissions and payment:** common process, category-specific exceptions, documents, verified scholarships, known charge types, deadlines, financing conditions and a link to the university's official refund/cancellation terms. Show “varies by program” where appropriate.
6. **Practical questions and alternatives:** a compact, university-specific FAQ, comparison routes and official contacts. Keep the previously removed **Campus & learner moments** section removed; it is not needed for this content expansion.

Move claim-specific source links next to the relevant section. The existing source rail can remain as an index, but a hostname alone does not tell a learner which fee, accreditation or admission rule it supports. Keep unverified ratings, learner counts and placement claims under the owner-approved deferral; do not enlarge them or create new trust badges from them.

## Content model and publishing workflow

New facts belong in reviewed CMS fields, not in another shared static template. Extend course and university revisions with distinct optional blocks for learning format, assessment/exams, fees and extras, documents, support, refund/source link, and institution identity. Each factual block should store `sourceUrl`, `checkedAt`, applicable program/institution, academic session and applicant category where relevant, reviewer and a state such as **verified / needs confirmation / not published**. A displayed freshness date must belong to the fact it accompanies; it must not be reset by a CSS or wording change.

Create the first full example for **Online MBA at MUJ, SMU and Amity**, where current official program pages and local fee evidence exist. Resolve conflicting facts and have an administrator review those three CMS proposals. Use the resulting layout/content pattern for BCA and MCA next, then all remaining programs and the three university profiles. Track a matrix of 30 courses × the sections above; a section is complete only when its displayed facts have a recorded disposition, including “unknown” where the university does not publish them. The pilot does not authorize bulk publication or importing other Online Manipal institutions.

## Intuitive layout and subtle motion acceptance

- Use a readable summary → details → next step hierarchy. Keep a 65–75-character reading column on desktop and one content column on phones. Long names, source URLs, tables and fee labels must wrap or use an explicitly labelled, keyboard-scrollable data region without moving the whole page sideways.
- Use existing components and spacing tokens. On a course or university page, only one contextual sticky action region is visible at a time. The section picker shows the active section, works with anchors and focus, and must not cover headings or the last field on phones. Limit primary navigation labels; group detail under them.
- Use 120–160ms color/border/shadow changes for hover/focus and 160–220ms for drawers or form-step state changes. If a card lifts, limit it to about 2–4px and preserve a visible keyboard focus outline. Accordion content must be available immediately when opened; any chevron motion is secondary to the state change. No parallax, autoplay, looping decoration, scroll hijacking or scroll-triggered hiding of content.
- Apply `prefers-reduced-motion` to **all** shared effects, including `.uv-fade-up`, card lift and skeleton shimmer. The current stylesheet disables lead-modal motion but does not consistently cover those effects. Content must be visible without waiting for animation or JavaScript; reserve dimensions to avoid layout shifts.
- Verify 320/360/390/768/1024/1280/1440px, 200% zoom, keyboard-only use, enlarged text, long program/source names and reduced motion. Test tab order, focus return, active section, horizontal overflow, source links, category switching, empty/unknown states and one production build. Track field performance after deployment; motion and extra content must not make pages feel slower.

No new generated images are required for this first pass. Reuse existing illustrations only where they clarify a learner task. University logos, certificate samples, faculty or campus photography and accreditation marks need authentic provenance/permission, not generated substitutes. If a later design requires a genuinely missing illustration, record only that new asset, its exact path, size and prompt in [ASSET_REQUIREMENTS_AND_PROMPTS.md](ASSET_REQUIREMENTS_AND_PROMPTS.md).
