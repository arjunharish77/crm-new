## International and NRI fees remain unresolved

The local browser and direct requests returned INR. Separate web retrieval of the same official BA page returned USD 3,000 base / USD 500 per semester; MAJMC returned USD 3,500 base / USD 880 per semester (USD 20 excess over four semesters). MAJMC’s web crawl was reported as three days old. These differing responses do not establish which fee applies to a particular applicant. No separate NRI schedule was verified.

Do not publish these USD observations as confirmed international or NRI prices. The other ten programs still need category-specific evidence. Fee-panel absence of additional charges is not evidence that they are waived.
