# Prepared curriculum drafts

30 September 2026. Thirty paraphrased semester overviews (twelve undergraduate, eighteen postgraduate) are available in the CMS revision form. They were prepared from the official curriculum sections recorded in the source review. These are topic summaries, not verbatim subject lists or complete syllabi. They remain unverified editorial drafts until reviewed for the relevant admission session.

## Use in CMS

Open **Courses → Edit → Propose or review revisions → Curriculum**. Expand **Preview prepared outline**, open the official source and inspect the semester summaries. **Load prepared outline** replaces the curriculum in the unsaved proposal, adds the source URL and records review context. Other fields and unsaved edits remain intact. This action does not create a saved revision, update the course or publish anything.

After reviewing/editing the terms, enter a reason and submit the normal proposal. Administrator review/application remains required. Loading marks curriculum verification as `generic`; it does not change publication status or certify source accuracy. Reload the page to discard an unsaved proposal.

## Deliberate limits

- MUJ/SMU BBA and MUJ B.Com elective groups remain choices; summaries do not combine all tracks into compulsory subjects. MUJ BCA electives also remain choices.
- Amity BCA includes provisional mini-project wording at the source. Its draft flags this rather than turning it into a confirmed requirement.
- Amity B.Com Honours requires awarding-institution and ACCA/non-ACCA route confirmation before publication.
- SMU BA and Amity BA summaries label their alternatives by subject combination/route. Shared introductory themes are distinguished from later alternatives. They do not select a learner’s combination or establish that every listed route is compulsory. Confirm current combination rules before applying a revision.
- Postgraduate elective paths remain choices, including dual MBA tracks and finance/marketing M.Com routes. MCA foundation/bridge requirements depend on prior qualifications.
- Amity M.Com follows its Financial Management page; confirm the generic record scope before publication. Amity MCA has source annotations requiring admissions-document confirmation.
- No draft here changes fees, eligibility or recognition claims. Full subject-level syllabi and admission-session confirmation remain pending for all drafts.

The prepared data lives in `src/data/prepared-curriculum-drafts.json`. Only the matching course draft is passed from the server into the private editor. Public catalog readers do not import this file. Full subject-level syllabi and current-session checks remain outstanding.
