## Owner decision (5 October 2026)

The owner states that these five programs are awarded by **Amity University Uttar Pradesh** (next-phase plan §11 #15), so the prepared results mark them as listed under it. The evidence JSON keeps the research status (`unclear`) as found. Public wording should name the listed institution and the owner's attribution rather than imply that the DEB document independently proves the awarding link. Before applying a proposal, open its official PDF link from the intended public environment and confirm the relevant row/period still resolves; a 5 October download does not prove that every public link remains reachable.
