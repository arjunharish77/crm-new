## Source and regional issues

- Earlier candidate links include unrelated MBA/BBA pages, a shared M.Com overview and an API endpoint. The register now supplies a dedicated `officialProgramUrl` for every course and preserves old candidates for traceability. Public source lists have not yet been changed.
- Amity's direct response in this environment returned INR prices. The web research tool returned USD variants or fetch errors for some identical canonical URLs. The USD variant must not be presented as a confirmed NRI quote without category and intake evidence. All 12 Amity international schedules remain unresolved.
- Amity MAJMC's web-tool USD view shows USD 3,500 total and USD 880 per semester: four instalments give USD 3,520. Several INR views also round semester amounts. Do not silently multiply rounded instalments or replace the stated total with a calculated amount.
- Amity M.Com is represented by the dedicated Financial Management page in this review. Confirm whether the generic catalog record should represent that specialization; do not combine its curriculum or price with the separate FinTech program.
- Promotional deadlines and discounts change frequently. They are not copied into the catalog by this review. Confirm application/exam/other charges and financing exclusions separately before describing any amount as all-inclusive.
- The [UGC-DEB program directory](https://deb.ugc.ac.in/Home/HEI_Prog_List) search index mixes academic years and learning modes; direct fetch failed during this review. Match the exact institution, program, online mode and admission session before changing recognition claims. Historical candidate PDFs remain historical evidence only.
